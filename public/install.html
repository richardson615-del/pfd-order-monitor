Setting up a tablet

For Premium staff. Restaurants never see this page and never download anything — the tablet arrives ready, and it updates itself.

How updates reach a tablet

Everything the restaurant sees is the live site: the dashboard, the alert gate, the branding. A deploy reaches every tablet on its own, at the next quiet moment (no order waiting, screen untouched for a minute). Nothing to install, ever, for that.

The only thing that ever needs a new install is the Android shell — the launcher icon, the app’s name, its Android permissions. That is pushed by the MDM, silently, to every enrolled tablet. A tablet that is behind shows one amber line on its dashboard (“This tablet needs an update from Premium”) and the office sees the same fact on the Devices page. The restaurant is never asked to do anything about it.

Enrolling a new tablet (MDM)

  1. Factory-reset the tablet, or start from the out-of-box screen.
  2. Enrol it into the MDM (QR code, or afw# on the Google sign-in screen, per the console’s instructions). The MDM applies the kiosk policy: single-app lock to Premium, auto-launch on boot, stay awake, portrait, Wi-Fi profile.
  3. The MDM installs the Premium app. Wait for it to appear — there is no download step.
  4. Open Premium. Sign in with the username and password from the CRM (or print them to the restaurant’s own printer from the Devices page).
  5. Tap Turn on alerts when the app asks, and allow it. If the MDM’s Chrome policy pre-grants notifications for pfd-order-monitor.vercel.app, this step does not appear.

Then, from the office

  1. Send a Test order from the CRM and check it reports reaching at least one device. Zero means alerts were not enabled.
  2. Press Accept on the tablet to stop the chiming. Only Accept stops it.
  3. Only once that works, hand the tablet to the restaurant.

Until the MDM hosts the build

Staff only. A tablet that is not yet enrolled can be set up by hand from this file, on the tablet itself: download, open the file from Chrome’s Downloads, tap Update or Install. This link goes away once the MDM console has the build.
